# The Adlam / Fulfulde partnership — round five

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, all
three track-wide policies included): **Fulɓe speakers translate,
we supply all tooling, nothing ships before their review**; no
deadline, any pace; credit, review authority, unshipping on
request; compensation discussed honestly; ownership settled in
writing first. Scaffold: `docs/i18n-glossary/ff.md`.

## Why Adlam

Two brothers invented an alphabet as teenagers so their language
could be written as itself, and thirty-five years later it is in
Unicode, on every major platform, and carried by a grassroots
literacy movement across the Sahel — if this track honors
community authority over language, Adlam is that principle in
script form. The technical ground (verified) is first-class:
native CLDR with Adlam digits and dates, riding RTL rails this
app has proven three languages deep. And the mutual-aid register
has local ground from herding-economy solidarity customs to the
careful line — met before in this project's Persian glossary —
between neighborly help and the benevolent-loan fund it must
never resemble.
