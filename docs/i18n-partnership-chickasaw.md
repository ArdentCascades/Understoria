# The Chickasaw partnership — round four

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, argued
in full there, both track-wide policies included): **Chickasaw
speakers translate, we supply all tooling, nothing ships before
their review**; no deadline, any pace; credit, review authority,
unshipping on request; compensation discussed honestly;
ownership settled in writing first. Scaffold:
`docs/i18n-glossary/cic.md`.

## Why Chickasaw

Very few first-language speakers remain — and around them, one
of the best-resourced revitalization programs in the world: the
Chickasaw Nation's Master-Apprentice program, Rosetta Stone
Chickasaw, children's clubs, and its own media operation. The
Master-Apprentice structure is, in miniature, this track's own
model — apprentices drafting under master speakers' review — so
the partnership may fit their existing practice unusually
naturally. The Choctaw track is this one's sibling (close
Muskogean relatives, shared removal history); each program
should know of the other's invitation.

## Who to contact

- **Chickasaw Nation Language Revitalization Program / Chickasaw

- **The Chickasaw Nation's education and culture divisions**, as
