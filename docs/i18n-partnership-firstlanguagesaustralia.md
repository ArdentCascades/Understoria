# The First Languages Australia umbrella — one conversation, many nations

## What this document is, and deliberately is not

The FirstVoices model (`docs/i18n-partnership-firstvoices.md`),
applied to Australia: hundreds of languages, each with its own
community, protocols, and revitalization state — from
full-immersion programs to reclamations-in-progress (Kaurna,
Wiradjuri, Noongar) to languages held under cultural protocols
that make outside questions inappropriate. **First Languages
Australia**, the national peak body for community-led language
work, is the right single door; individual nations are reached
only through channels they control.

Per both track-wide policies (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`):
**no Australian language gets a scaffold, a published probe, or
a candidate-list entry unless a community chooses it** — and
Australian ICIP protocols, avoidance registers, and gendered or
initiatory knowledge are exactly the access-by-choice cases the
policy names: any community's access rules are terms we accept
as given, never negotiate.

## What we would offer any community FLA connects us with

The standing model, unchanged: speakers translate, we supply all
tooling, nothing ships before their review; no deadline, any
pace; credit, review authority, unshipping on request theirs;
compensation discussed honestly; licensing and ICIP-respecting
materials ownership settled in writing first. Technically, the
sibling tracks' verified groundwork covers the region's needs in
principle (orthography-specific characters, no-CLDR fallback
patterns, honest disclosure), and AIATSIS-style protocols would
be followed where they apply.

## Who to contact

- **First Languages Australia** — the national peak body; the
  door.
- Through FLA, as they judge appropriate — never directly and
  cold from us — regional language centres and community
  programs.
