# The Guaraní partnership — round four

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, both
track-wide policies included): **Guaraní speakers translate, we
supply all tooling, nothing ships before their review**; no
deadline, any pace; credit, review authority, unshipping on
request; compensation discussed honestly; ownership settled in
writing first. Scaffold: `docs/i18n-glossary/gn.md` — including
its scope note: this track addresses Paraguayan Guaraní; the
Indigenous Guaraní peoples' varieties are their own communities'
to ask for, never ours to assume.

## Why Guaraní

The track's unique profile: a majority-spoken, co-official
national language — millions of speakers, taught in every
Paraguayan school — that is nonetheless digitally under-served
and carries a live history of stigma. A mutual-aid platform
whose Spanish edition is already complete can meet Paraguay's
actual bilingualism honestly: `gn → es → en`, with the register
question (warm Jopara vs formal Guaraniete) put where it belongs
— with the Academia, the Ateneo tradition, and working teachers.
And the lexicon fits: jopói (reciprocal giving), minga (the work
day), ñande (the inclusive we).

## Who to contact

- **Academia de la Lengua Guaraní** and the **Secretaría de
  Políticas Lingüísticas** (Paraguay's language-policy
  secretariat) — the statutory doors.
- **Ateneo de Lengua y Cultura Guaraní** — the teaching
  institution with nationwide reach.
- **Yvy Marãe'ỹ** (San Lorenzo) — the language institute active
  in translation and terminology.
- **Universidad Nacional de Asunción** Guaraní programs.
