# The Hawaiian partnership — round three

## The model

The standing partnership model
(`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, argued in full there,
including the track-wide never-scaffold-unbidden policy):
**Hawaiian speakers translate, we supply all tooling, and
nothing ships before their review** — the whole technical burden
on us; no deadline, any pace; credit, review authority, and
unshipping on request theirs; compensation discussed honestly in
the first conversation; licensing and materials ownership
settled in writing first. The scaffold is
`docs/i18n-glossary/haw.md`.

## Why Hawaiian

- **The proof that revitalization works**: from a few hundred
  child speakers in the early 1980s to a K-through-doctorate
  Hawaiian-medium pipeline — ʻAha Pūnana Leo's nests (modeled on
  kōhanga reo; this track's Māori outreach is this one's
  sibling), the kula kaiapuni immersion schools, and UH Hilo's
  Hawaiian-medium college.
- **The easiest technical ground on the track**: Hawaiian is a
  first-class CLDR locale (native plurals, dates rendering
  «Poʻalua, 15 Kepakemapa» for free, the endonym with its ʻokina
  supplied), settled orthography, no rendering risk.
- **The vocabulary fits like it was waiting**: kuleana for the
  claim/"In my care" family, kōkua for help, laulima for the
  work day, kākou for the app's inclusive "we" — all flagged in
  the scaffold as anchors and discussion items, all speakers'
  calls.

## Who to contact

- **ʻAha Pūnana Leo** — the language nests and their statewide
  infrastructure.
- **Ka Haka ʻUla O Keʻelikōlani College of Hawaiian Language**
  (UH Hilo) — the Hawaiian-medium college; also home to the
  lexicon/terminology tradition.
- **Kawaihuelani Center for Hawaiian Language** (UH Mānoa).
- **Kamehameha Schools** — Hawaiian culture-based education at
  scale.
- **The Office of Hawaiian Affairs** — for the civic framing,
  where useful.
