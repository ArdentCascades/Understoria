# The Hmong partnership — round five

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, all
three track-wide policies included): **Hmong speakers translate,
we supply all tooling, nothing ships before their review**; no
deadline, any pace; credit, review authority, unshipping on
request; compensation discussed honestly; ownership settled in
writing first. Scaffold: `docs/i18n-glossary/hmn.md`.

## Why Hmong

Because the institutions already exist and already have the
right name: the diaspora's **Mutual Assistance Associations**
have organized Hmong-to-Hmong help in the United States for
fifty years. A mutual-aid platform offering itself to Hmong
communities is not proposing an idea — it is offering a tool to
people who invented the institutional form the app imitates.
The language fits as neatly: the reciprocal grammar (*sib*)
names mutual help — *kev sib pab* — in ordinary, warm speech.
And technically the ground is simple: the RPA is pure ASCII,
zero rendering risk, on the proven no-ICU rails.

- **Hmong studies programs** — Concordia University St. Paul's
  Center for Hmong Studies, UW-Madison's HMoob program,
  Fresno/Sacramento State programs.
