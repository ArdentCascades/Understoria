# The INALI / Mexico umbrella — one door for sixty-eight language groups

## What this document is, and deliberately is not

The FirstVoices model (`docs/i18n-partnership-firstvoices.md`),
applied to Mexico: **sixty-eight recognized Indigenous language
groups** (hundreds of variants — Nahuatl, the Mixtec and Zapotec
continuums, Maya, P'urhépecha, Otomí/Hñähñu, Mixe, Wixárika and
many more), all *national languages* under the 2003 General Law
of Linguistic Rights, with **INALI** — the Instituto Nacional de
Lenguas Indígenas — as the statutory institute, alongside
community academies (the Ve'e Tu'un Savi for Mixtec among them)
and university programs. One conversation at the umbrella;
individual communities reached only through channels they
recognize.

Per all three track-wide policies
(`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`): **no Mexican Indigenous
language gets a scaffold, a published probe, or a candidate-list
entry unless its community chooses it.** Official recognition
makes doors findable — sixty-eight of them — not ours to walk
through uninvited.

## What we would offer any community INALI connects us with

The standing model, unchanged — speakers translate, we supply
all tooling, nothing ships before their review; compensation
discussed honestly; ownership settled in writing first — plus
two region-specific notes:

- **The Spanish bridge is already built**: the app ships
  complete Spanish, so any Mexican Indigenous language gets the
  `→ es → en` fallback chain on proven rails and the full
  Spanish corpus as a translators' working aid (the Quechua and
  K'iche' tracks' precedent).
- **The register ground is the region's own**: tequio, faena,
  guelaguetza, mano vuelta — Mexico's communal-labor and
  reciprocity institutions are living practice with names in
  both Spanish and the languages themselves; every glossary
  scaffold this track ever builds here starts from vocabulary
  the communities maintain, not concepts to explain.

## Who to contact

- **INALI** (Ciudad de México) — the statutory institute; the
  door.
- Through INALI, as they judge appropriate: the community
  language academies, the intercultural universities, and
  community organizations.
