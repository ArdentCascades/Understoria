# The K'iche' partnership — round four

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, both
track-wide policies included): **K'iche' speakers translate, we
supply all tooling, nothing ships before their review**; no
deadline, any pace; credit, review authority, unshipping on
request; compensation discussed honestly; ownership settled in
writing first. Scaffold: `docs/i18n-glossary/quc.md`.

## Why K'iche'

- The Mayan family's entry: a million speakers, a statutory
  academy (the **ALMG**, whose unified alphabet is official
  across Guatemala's Mayan languages — the institutional door
  the Inuktitut track proved), and the **Popol Wuj** as a
  literary credential no other language on this track carries.
- The Spanish bridge: like Quechua, `quc → es → en`, with the
  app's complete Spanish edition as a working aid for
  translators.
- And soberly: the safety fences this app is built on — no
  rosters, no reports to authority, identity the member holds —
  carry their heaviest historical weight here, and deserve
  their most careful translation.

## Who to contact

- **Academia de Lenguas Mayas de Guatemala (ALMG)** — the
  statutory academy; its K'iche' linguistic community
  (comunidad lingüística K'iche').
- **Universidad Rafael Landívar** (Instituto de Lingüística) and
  **Universidad del Valle** Mayan-language programs.
- Mayan-language education organizations (PLFM — Proyecto
  Lingüístico Francisco Marroquín; OKMA's successor circles) as
  doors open.
