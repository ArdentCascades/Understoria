# The Sámi partnership — round four

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, both
track-wide policies included): **Sámi speakers translate, we
supply all tooling, nothing ships before their review**; no
deadline, any pace; credit, review authority, unshipping on
request; compensation discussed honestly; ownership settled in
writing first. Scaffold: `docs/i18n-glossary/se.md` (Northern
Sámi first as the largest; sibling Sámi languages per-tag on
their communities' initiative).

## Why Sámi — and why we arrive as the junior partner

Sámi language technology is the best minority-language
infrastructure in the world: Giellatekno and Divvun at the
Arctic University of Norway build analyzers, spellcheckers, and
keyboards with and for Sámi institutions, and the Sámi
parliaments' joint terminology body (Sámi Giellagáldu) already
answers exactly the questions our scaffolds pose. This track
arrives offering interoperation, not novelty — our tooling
follows their conventions, never duplicates their work. The
technical ground (verified) matches: Northern Sámi is a
first-class CLDR locale with a grammatical dual our gates
already handle, native dates free, and settled orthography —
the easiest wiring tier on the track.

## Who to contact

- **Sámi Giellagáldu** — the joint Nordic terminology authority
  under the Sámi parliaments.
- **Divvun / Giellatekno** (UiT, Tromsø) — the language
  technology groups; also the right reviewers of our model
  itself.
- **The Sámi parliaments** (Norway, Sweden, Finland) — language
  departments, as the channels they prefer.
- **Sámi allaskuvla / Sámi University of Applied Sciences**
  (Guovdageaidnu) — the Sámi-language higher-education
  institution.
