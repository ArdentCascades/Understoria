# The Western Armenian partnership — round five

The standing model (`docs/i18n-partnership-cherokee.md`, all
three track-wide policies included): **Western Armenian speakers
translate, we supply all tooling, nothing ships before their
review**; no deadline, any pace; credit, review authority,
unshipping on request; compensation discussed honestly;
ownership settled in writing first. Scaffold:
`docs/i18n-glossary/hyw.md`.

## Why Western Armenian

The track's first diaspora-endangered language: endangered not
because Armenian is dying but because this branch's homeland
was destroyed in 1915, and a century of diaspora means every
generation chooses the language anew. The choosing
infrastructure exists — worldwide school networks, the
Mekhitarist tradition, dedicated foundation revitalization
programs — and a mutual-aid app fits the diaspora's own
institutional history (the agoump, the compatriotic unions)
while drawing, with the community's precision, the line the
app draws everywhere: level help between neighbors, never
benefaction from above.

## Who to contact

- **The Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation's Armenian Communities
  department** — the major funder of Western Armenian
  revitalization (teacher training, immersive programs); the
  best-networked single door.
- **INALCO's Armenian studies** (Paris) and **Haigazian
  University** (Beirut).
- **The diaspora school networks** (AGBU's and the
  congregations' schools, Istanbul's Armenian schools) through
  their educational offices.
- **The Armenian Communities' cultural associations** (Hamazkayin,
  Tekeyan) — the agoump networks themselves.
